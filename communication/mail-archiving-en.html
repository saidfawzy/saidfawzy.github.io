Mail Archiving System (English Edition)

An advanced engineering guide to architecting, deploying, and utilizing an enterprise email retention and search system. Learn how to seamlessly connect Outlook, Excel, Access, and Power BI to securely catalog, query, and safeguard mission-critical corporate correspondence.

This hands-on walkthrough breaks down the end-user operations, database components, installation procedures, and standalone native Outlook archiving techniques to ensure continuous business compliance and data preservation.

Comprehensive end-user operational manual: navigating the search interface, executing multi-parameter queries by sender, date range, and keyword, as well as securely inspecting and exporting archived correspondence attachments.

Part 4: Archiving Mails without using the system

Watch & Learn
▼

Manual and native archiving techniques: harnessing built-in Outlook capabilities, configuring PST storage files, automating sorting via custom Rules and Quick Steps, and exporting data tables directly to Excel for ad-hoc auditing.
